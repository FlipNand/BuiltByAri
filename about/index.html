Built by Ari started with a group of Toronto students who had been building for the web since before it was a job.

We grew up making things for the internet: school projects, small business sites for family friends, a lot of code that never saw daylight. When local businesses started asking for help, we turned it into a studio.

The pitch has not changed since. Instead of buying a template and bending it into shape, we design and write the site for the business in front of us — modern code, honest pricing, and someone who picks up the phone after launch.

Working with us also means something to the people behind it: the studio is run by students and early-career developers in Toronto, and the money you spend goes to local people learning the trade properly.

We are small on purpose. Fewer projects at once means every site gets the attention a $10,000 agency build would get, at a price a café on Bloor can actually carry.

01 Principles

Four rules we do not break.

These are the promises behind every plan. If we ever break one, we would rather hear about it than have you quietly live with it.

  1. 01

    No templates

    Every layout is designed for the business it belongs to. Nothing is assembled from a theme library and recoloured.

  2. 02

    No hostage code

    Your domain, content, and code are yours. Ask for the files and you get them, with a short handover document explaining how they fit together.

  3. 03

    No dark patterns

    One price, quoted in writing before work starts. No auto-renew trap, no “call for pricing”, no upsell for accessibility.

  4. 04

    No shortcuts on access

    Every site is built to WCAG 2.2 AA and tested with a keyboard and a screen reader. Accessible sites are also faster and rank better — there is no trade-off.
